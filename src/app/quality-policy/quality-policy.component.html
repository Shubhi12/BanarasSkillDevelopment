CORPORATE QUALITY POLICY

Banaras Skill Development India Private Ltd. will meet its statement of mission by:

  • Committing to provide Quality Education, Training and related services to the students/customers/partners in the      areas of Information Technology/Accounts & Finance/Management/Soft-Skills, ensuring customer delight and          satisfaction of interested parties.
  • Involving industry in both the Development and Evolution of our training programs and courses.
  • Ensuring that programs and services are designed and implemented with a commitment to meeting student/customer requirements.
  • Ensuring that standards are maintained and improved by active monitoring, reviewing and improving all activities.
