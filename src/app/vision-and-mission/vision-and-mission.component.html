VISION AND MISSION

VISION

To enable ‘Learning’ bring a visible & positive impact to individuals & organizations. To be the reliable ‘Solution Providers’ for individuals seeking skill enhancement for employability.

MISSION

Banaras Skill was founded to provide cost-effective & result-oriented services in Education & Training, in consonance with it’s slogan – Excel with Technology , Empower with Education. We strive to become a leading organization providing skill-enhancement training to individuals & training solutions to organizations. To achieve this, we ensure selection of right talent & constant grooming & motivation for self-satisfaction & improvement, make available facilities which enable efficient & productive output from team members & taking care of customers by satisfying their needs in totality.
